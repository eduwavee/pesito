# Comparador (home + resultados)

Scope: `/` — empty state + results. Mode: Operate. Audience: Argentine hardware buyers (PC builders), public, and portfolio viewers. Job: find the cheapest real offer across six stores in seconds and click through. Success balances an instant verdict with the felt live race between stores. Avoid: generic dark SaaS, loud gamer look, looking like a store, empty minimalism.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The cheapest price is written as the Argentine fluo price card, and corrected live (struck through, rewritten) each time another store beats it. Refuses the category default of a grid of equal product cards on dark neon.

OWN-WORLD: Cool off-white card stock ground, ink-black text, fluo pink card (#FF3D8B family) reserved for the verdict only, fluo lime for "winner" marks on the price strip; store colors only as round identity marks next to store names. Archivo variable: condensed heavy for prices, normal width for UI. Hairline rules, flat sheets, no glow.

STORY: Visitor types a component, sees the stores answer one by one, watches the card get struck and rewritten until it settles, trusts it via the price strip and per-store minimums, then clicks to the store.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (brand left, search center, Sync credit right). Store status rail. Left: rotated fluo card with monumental condensed price, store, title, "Ver en tienda". Right: price strip (all offers as dots on one $ axis) + per-store minimum table. Results list starts below the fold edge.

FORM: Cartel flúo, #6 on ordered list (raised by lexicon strike-and-rewrite, star-atlas price strip, video-feed winner ownership, airport monumental scale). Seed key 8617f8bd.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
